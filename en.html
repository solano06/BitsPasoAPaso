Frequently asked questions

Is my input data sent to a server? +

No. Every calculator in BitsPasoAPaso runs 100% client-side in pure JavaScript, ensuring complete privacy and instant results.

How does Study Mode work? +

Study Mode hides the final summary and lets you reveal intermediate steps one by one, making it ideal for self-testing before checking the final answer.

Can I share a solved exercise with classmates or students? +

Yes. Every calculation syncs with URL parameters; clicking "Copy link" gives you a direct URL that loads the exact same problem.

How accurate are the IEEE-754 and Two's Complement engines? +

They rely on native binary buffers (ArrayBuffer/DataView) and arbitrary-precision BigInt math for exact 32-bit and 64-bit representations.
