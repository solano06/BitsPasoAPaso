Frequently asked questions

Why does standard ASCII stop at 127? +

It uses 7 bits (2^7 = 128 codes, 0x00 to 0x7F), typically stored in 8-bit bytes.

What separates A (65) from a (97)? +

Exactly 32 (bit 5: 01000001 vs 01100001), making case conversion a single bit flip.

What are codes 0–31 and 127? +

Non-printable control characters such as NUL (0), TAB (9), LF (10), CR (13), ESC (27), and DEL (127).
