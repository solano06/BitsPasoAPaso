Privacy Policy

At BitsPasoAPaso we respect your privacy. All inputs entered into our calculators are processed locally in your browser and never transmitted to external servers.

Recent exercise history, light/dark theme preference, and Study Mode state are stored strictly in your device's localStorage.

We use privacy-friendly, cookieless aggregated analytics (Vercel Web Analytics) solely to monitor technical performance. For privacy questions, contact contacto@bitspasoapaso.com.

Frequently asked questions

Do you store my IP addresses or exercises in a database? +

No. No form input ever leaves your browser.

How do I clear my calculation history? +

Click "Clear" on the recent history bar of any calculator or clear site data in your browser.

Do you use advertising or tracking cookies? +

No, we do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

Where can I send privacy inquiries? +

You can contact us anytime at contacto@bitspasoapaso.com.
